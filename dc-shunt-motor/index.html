Electrical Machines

1. Introduction

A DC shunt motor is an electrical machine in which the field winding is connected in parallel with the armature winding. The motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy. This Virtual Laboratory allows students to investigate the effect of armature voltage on the speed and electrical parameters of a DC shunt motor.

2. Learning Objectives

  • Understand the operating principle of a DC shunt motor.
  • Identify the parallel connection between armature and field winding.
  • Measure armature voltage and field voltage.
  • Measure armature current and field current.
  • Investigate the relationship between armature voltage and motor speed.

3. Theory

In a DC shunt motor, the armature winding and shunt field winding are connected in parallel across the DC supply. For a constant field flux, the motor speed is approximately proportional to the armature voltage. Therefore:

Armature Voltage ↑ → Motor Speed ↑

5. Experimental Procedure

  1. Set the armature voltage to 0 V.
  2. Click START.
  3. Gradually increase the armature voltage.
  4. Observe the armature voltmeter.
  5. Observe the field voltmeter.
  6. Observe the armature ammeter.
  7. Observe the field ammeter.
  8. Observe the line ammeter.
  9. Record the motor speed and torque.
  10. Repeat for different voltage values.
  11. Click STOP when finished.

6. Safety Note

This Virtual Laboratory is a computer simulation intended for educational purposes. Always follow the appropriate safety procedures when operating an actual DC motor laboratory setup.
