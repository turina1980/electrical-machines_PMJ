Direct-On-Line (DOL) Starter Virtual Laboratory

Electrical Machines · Control Circuit Wiring Practice and Simulation

1. Introduction and Learning Objectives

The Direct-On-Line (DOL) starter connects a motor directly to the supply through a contactor. In this virtual lab, students practise wiring the control circuit using clickable terminal overlays on the original training-panel photographs supplied by the lecturer.

Scope: This activity follows the control/ladder circuit supplied by the lecturer. It simulates contactor latching, START/STOP push buttons, thermal-overload contacts and pilot lamps. It is an educational simulation, not a substitute for supervised practical work on a real trainer.

Learning objectives

  • Identify the START NO and STOP NC push-button contacts.
  • Connect the contactor coil and NO auxiliary holding contact.
  • Connect overload NC (95–96) and trip NO (97–98) contacts.
  • Demonstrate motor ON and motor trip indicator operation.

5. Safety Reminder

This virtual lab is for learning the control-circuit sequence. Before wiring a real DOL starter, isolate the supply, verify absence of voltage, follow the lab's risk assessment and wiring diagram, and get the lecturer or laboratory supervisor to check the circuit before energising it.

Terminal mapping note: The visible trainer labels have been mapped to the reference as follows: PB3 START NO (M8/N8), PB4 STOP NC (P8/Q8), KM1 coil A1/A2 (M1/N1), KM1 NO auxiliary 43/44 (J1/K1), overload NC 95/96 (P2/Q2), overload NO 97/98 (J2/K2). Please verify these against the actual trainer before physical use.
